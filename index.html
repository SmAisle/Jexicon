How to Play

  • Each day: a theme and 5 cards, easy to hard.
  • Read the definition, tap Spell it, and type the word one letter at a time.
  • Right letters turn green. One wrong letter or 20 seconds up and you’re out.
  • Pass all 5, then try the ⚡ bonus: one more word in 15 seconds.
  • Always the full word: refrigerator, not fridge.

New game at midnight Eastern. Past days are in the Archive. By playing, you agree to the smAisle Terms and Privacy. Opens and plays are counted anonymously.
