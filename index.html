How to Play

Every day there's a new theme and 5 cards. Each card shows the definition of a word that fits the theme.

  • Read the definition, then tap Spell it.
  • Type the word one letter at a time. The card tells you how many letters it has.
  • Every right letter turns green. Spell the whole word to pass the card and move on to the next one.
  • One wrong letter and the game is over. No take-backs.
  • Answers are always the full word, never a short form: refrigerator, not fridge.
  • The cards get harder as you go. Pass all 5 for a perfect day.
  • ⚡ Bonus round: after the 5 cards, try one more word against a 20-second clock.

A new game comes out every day at midnight Eastern time. Past days are in the Archive. Want more? Practice (in the menu) lets you play other themes. Practice games aren’t counted.

By playing, you agree to the smAisle Terms and Privacy. The game counts opens and plays anonymously to see how many people play. No names, no accounts.
